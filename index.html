§ 01 — WHO WE ARE

A robotics team that happens to be a public high school.

Newtown High School sits in Elmhurst — a neighborhood where the students collectively speak dozens of languages. Every winter a chunk of them spend their nights over a CAD model, a soldering iron, and a half-built drivetrain.

We're the Newtown Technotics — FRC Team 1635. Each January, FIRST releases the season's game; six weeks later a 120-pound robot rolls off our shop floor. In between is the part the team is really about: learning mechanical engineering, electrical, software and project management against a deadline that does not move.

§ 05 — BY THE NUMBERS

Twenty-one years, one Queens public school.

  • 2005

    Rookie season

    Team 1635 fields its first robot — the start of an unbroken run of FRC seasons out of Newtown High School.

  • '05–'26

    21 straight seasons

    One public school, twenty-one consecutive years of designing, building and competing every single winter.

  • HOME

    NYC Regional · Armory

    Our home event runs at the historic Fort Washington Avenue Armory under the NYC FIRST banner.

  • ROSTER

    ~50 students

    Mechanical, electrical, programming, design and business — plus faculty and engineer mentors.

  • REACH

    Queens & beyond

    Demos and workshops in schools across the borough, recruiting the next generation of builders.

  • VALUE

    Gracious professionalism

    FIRST's core value, lived in the pit — we help the teams next to us as readily as we compete with them.
