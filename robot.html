Sheet 02 of 05 Season 2026 — Rebuilt

Meet AJ.

Custom six-wheel base. Compliant-roller intake. Two-stage elevator, articulated arm and a twin-flywheel launcher. Every part drawn, cut and bolted by students.

Take it apart

Stage 01 · Assembled

120 pounds, fully dressed.

Competition configuration. Bumpers on, battery in, inspected and cleared for the field.

Stage 02 · Superstructure

Elevator, arm and turret lift away.

Everything above the frame rail bolts on as one superstructure — designed so it can come off in the pit in under four minutes.

Stage 03 · Powertrain

Six wheels, four NEOs, one belt run.

Motors sit inboard on the gearbox plate. Power routes out to the corners through belt-in-tube modules.

Stage 04 · Fully exploded

Every sub-assembly, separated.

Chassis, drive, motors, elevator, arm, turret and intake — the way the CAD model looks the night before manufacturing starts.

§ 02 — Subsystems

Drawn before it was cut.

Autodesk Fusion and Inventor. Every part toleranced against stock we actually have in the shop.

§ 03 — In the pit

Nothing survives first contact with a match.

Bag-and-tag is gone, but the routine is not.

Load in Friday morning, pass inspection, then keep a laptop and a soldering iron within reach for two straight days. Somebody is always under the robot.

Every mount on AJ was designed so it can be reached with a robot on its side and six minutes on the clock. That constraint shaped the whole frame layout.

§ 04 — Back catalogue

Twenty-one machines.

Each one named, photographed, and retired to the back wall of the shop.
