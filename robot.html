Meet "AJ".

The machine designed, built and driven by 1635 Technotics this season — a custom drivetrain, a compliant-roller intake, and a launcher tower.

§ 01 — OVERVIEW

Engineered to win.

AJ is this year's competition robot — designed in CAD, machined and 3D-printed in the Newtown HS shop, and wrapped in red bumpers reading 16 and 35.

A six-wheel custom base carries the weight; a roller stack intake feeds a flywheel launcher tower up top. Every subsystem started as a wood-and-extrusion prototype before becoming the parts you see on the field.
