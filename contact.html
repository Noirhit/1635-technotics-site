§ 01 — JOIN US

You don't need to know anything. You need to show up.

Newtown HS students from any grade can join. We'll teach you to mill, to wirings, to write code, and to debug and fix the robot for the next one. Find the team in room B2 in the basement.

  • ● Open to all Newtown HS students, grades 9–12
  • ● No experience required — we teach you everything
  • ● Build season runs January – February (six intense weeks)
  • ● Mechanical, electrical, programming, design or business
  • ● Mentors welcome year-round — engineers, alumni, parents
  • ● Sponsor inquiries: ask for the sponsor packet
