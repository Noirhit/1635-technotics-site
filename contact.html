§ 01 — Join the team

You do not need to know anything.

Newtown HS students from any grade can join. We will teach you to mill, to solder, to write autonomous code, and to lose a match gracefully and have the robot fixed before the next one.

  • Open to all Newtown HS students, grades 9–12
  • No experience required — we teach you everything
  • Build season runs January to February, six intense weeks
  • Mechanical, electrical, programming, design or business
  • Mentors welcome year round — engineers, alumni, parents
  • Sponsors: ask for the packet and we will send it the same day
